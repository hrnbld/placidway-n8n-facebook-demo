Frequently Asked Questions
What is liposuction, and how does it work?
Liposuction is a cosmetic surgery that removes excess fat from specific areas of the body. It works by using a cannula to suction out fat cells, helping to shape and contour the body. It's ideal for areas like the abdomen, thighs, and arms that don't respond well to diet or exercise.

How much weight can I expect to lose after liposuction?
Liposuction is not a weight loss procedure but a body contouring treatment. The amount of fat removed depends on the patient's needs, but most people see significant improvements in body shape, not a drastic weight reduction.

Is liposuction safe?
Yes, liposuction is generally considered safe when performed by a qualified surgeon. However, like all surgeries, it carries some risks, including infection, scarring, or uneven fat removal. Be sure to choose an experienced and reputable surgeon like Dr. Arturo Valdez.

What is the recovery time for liposuction?
Most patients can return to work within 1-2 weeks after liposuction, although full recovery may take 4-6 weeks. Swelling and bruising are normal and should subside over time.

How long do the results of liposuction last?
The results of liposuction are permanent as long as the patient maintains a stable weight and healthy lifestyle. However, weight gain can affect the results, so it's essential to follow a balanced diet and exercise routine post-surgery.
