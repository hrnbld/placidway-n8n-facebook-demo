Understanding the Triple Vessel Bypass Procedure
Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting is an open-heart procedure designed to treat severe coronary artery disease. When atherosclerotic plaque accumulates inside the blood vessels supplying the heart muscle, oxygenated blood flow becomes critically restricted, leading to angina, shortness of breath, or heart attacks. In a triple vessel bypass, three primary blocked coronary arteries are bypassed using healthy blood vessel conduits harvested from the patient's chest, arm, or leg.
During the procedure at King Faisal Specialist Hospital & Research Centre, cardiac surgeons operate in specialized surgical suites supported by dedicated cardiac anesthesia and perfusion teams. Bypassing the obstructed arterial segments restores necessary oxygenation directly to the myocardium, working to relieve persistent chest symptoms, enhance cardiac output, and support long-term heart stability.
